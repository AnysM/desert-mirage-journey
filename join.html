The journey

Theme of the year: 間 Ma,
the space in between.

Day 1

Marrakech

Airport pickup, a night in a riad of the medina.

Days 2–8

Into the desert

Across the Atlas to the Sahara. Nomadic trekking, bivouac under the stars, embodied practice, silence.

Days 9–10

What emerges

At the fixed camp: co-creation, performance, integration.

Days 11–12

Return

Back to Marrakech. Departure on December 21.

Who holds the space

Two artists who know this desert.

Yi Chen

Butoh-inspired dancer, performance artist and curator. Co-directed the Space Between Butoh Festival in Bern. Co-creator of Desert Mirage Journey since its first edition.

Anys

Painter, dancer, guide of desert retreats and initiatory journeys. Franco-Moroccan. The Sahara is not a destination for him, it is a place of return.
