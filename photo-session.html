Hugo Martial Photographer & filmmaker

Hugo Martial devotes himself to exploring and sharing wonder and mystery.
As a photographer, he works with the worlds of lifestyle and wellbeing.
He founded vivres, a place of residency between art, awareness and nature, and photographed and filmed its story for years. He travels with Desert Mirage Journey and documents the journey.
